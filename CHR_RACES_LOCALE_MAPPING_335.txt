ChrRaces.dbc locale mapping - WoW 3.3.5a
===========================================

Each localized name is a 16-offset locstring followed by a mask.
Actual client locale slot order used by 3.3.5a:
  0 enUS
  1 koKR
  2 frFR
  3 deDE
  4 zhCN
  5 zhTW
  6 esES
  7 esMX
  8 ruRU
  9-15 reserved/unused here

The previous CSV header incorrectly treated enGB/enCN/enTW as separate runtime
slots. That shifted French into the German slot, which is why a deDE client
showed French race names. The active ChrRaces.dbc and this CSV are now aligned
to the real client slots.
