Ogre Lua camera test
====================
This build deliberately tests the CharacterCreate ModelFFX camera path.
For Ogre only, CharacterCreate:SetCamera(1) is forced AFTER every
UpdateCustomizationScene() call. Other races remain on camera 0.

This is an experimental diagnostic fix. If the Ogre framing changes,
we have confirmed the active control point is CharacterCreate.lua and
can tune the correct camera next. If it does not change at all, camera
selection is being controlled natively and further Lua scale/position
changes are not useful.
